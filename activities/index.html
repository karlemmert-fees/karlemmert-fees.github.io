supervision

I supervise doctoral students working on simulation modeling and policy evaluation, as well as bachelor’s and master’s theses in public health, epidemiology, and health economics. I also run hands-on workshops on microsimulation modeling with IMPACT NCD Germany. Prospective students are welcome to get in touch.
